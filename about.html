About WEEKEND

Making it easier for Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar to get out and play.

What is WEEKEND?

WEEKEND is a sports venue discovery and booking website. A user can browse turfs and courts, compare prices, choose a convenient time slot and confirm a booking in minutes.

Why we started

People who want to play cricket, football or badminton on weekends usually have to call different grounds one by one to check which slot is free and how much it costs.

A simpler way to play

One website where all nearby venues, their sports, prices and time slots are listed together, with a simple booking flow from venue selection to confirmation.

What you can do
  • Venue search, filter by sport / location / price / rating and sorting
  • Compare venue facilities, sports, opening hours and prices
  • Booking form with date, duration (1–5 hours) and time slot selection
  • See a clear total based on your selected duration
  • Choose UPI, Card or Pay at Venue
  • Booking confirmation with a generated booking ID
  • My Bookings page with Upcoming / Completed / Cancelled tabs and cancel option
  • Manage your account and booking history
Why players choose WEEKEND
  • Venues across Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar
  • Upfront hourly pricing
  • Quick slot selection
  • Multiple payment choices
  • Easy booking management
